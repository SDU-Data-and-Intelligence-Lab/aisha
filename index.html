About me

I am an Associate Professor in SDU Software Engineering with a long-standing connection to the University of Southern Denmark. My background is in software engineering and agent-based systems, with earlier research focusing on coordination, resource allocation, and process performance in complex distributed infrastructures.

I defended my PhD at SDU in 2018. My subsequent work has continued this line through cyber-physical systems, virtual power plants, distributed energy resources, and multi-objective optimisation.

More recently, my work has increasingly connected this systems perspective with digital education and AI-supported learning environments. I lead the project “Scalable Exams at SDU for Digital Skills,” which responds to the challenges that generative AI creates for traditional assessment formats. The project develops secure and scalable hands-on exam environments using SDU’s UCloud infrastructure, with the aim of supporting practical assessment of digital skills.
